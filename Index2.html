Phase 6: Debriefing & PMBOK Framework

Bridging Simulation to Real-World Project Management

Project Triple Constraints

In standard project management theory (PMBOK), Scope, Time, and Cost form the core constraints triangle. Any unapproved shift in Mr. Ahmad's scope directly impacts the RM 500,000 budget and the 5-month deadline.

Preventing Scope Creep

Scope Creep occurs when features or work items are added informally without adjusting cost or timeline. Documenting explicit Limits & Exclusions serves as the contractor's primary legal and operational shield.

Core Takeaway: The signed Project Scope Statement between Royal Construction Sdn. Bhd. and Mr. Ahmad establishes the official Scope Baseline for all future evaluations and review gates.

Reference Notes: 6 Scope Statement Elements

1. Project Objective:

Defines clear project goals bounded by time (5 Months) and budget (RM 500,000) for Mr. Ahmad's residential house in Iskandar Puteri.

2. Deliverables:

Physical end products including completed house structure, Certificate of Completion and Compliance (CCC), and as-built manuals.

3. Milestones:

Critical timeline checkpoints: MBIP Approval, Foundation & Substructure, Main Superstructure, Roofing & Finishes, Handover.

4. Technical Requirements:

Concrete strength grades (C30), SIRIM electrical standards, sanitary plumbing specifications, and MBIP local council codes.

5. Limits & Exclusions:

Crucial boundaries! Explicitly stating items NOT included in the RM500k budget (e.g. loose furniture, luxury landscaping, pools).

6. Customer Reviews:

Formal review checkpoints with Mr. Ahmad at the conclusion of each milestone stage to confirm quality and sign off changes.
